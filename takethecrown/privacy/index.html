Take the Crown

Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

In short

  • Your hero, progress, purchases and settings stay on your device.
  • The developer does not collect, receive, or have access to any of it.
  • Take the Crown has no account, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.
  • Achievements are handled by Apple Game Center, and signing in is optional.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to Take the Crown (the "App"), listed on the App Store as Take the Crown RPG, for iPhone and iPad, created by Alexander Rohr (the "Developer"). The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Information stored on your device

Take the Crown stores your saved game locally on your device using standard iOS storage. This includes your hero and the name you give them, your stats, level, gold, gear and potions, the levels you have cleared, your quests, enemy cards and trophies, and your settings for music, sound and vibration. This information stays on the device it was created on; the App does not sync it to iCloud or any other device.

Take the Crown does not operate any servers, and the App does not send your saved game or play activity to the Developer.

Game Center

Take the Crown can report your achievements to Apple Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, the achievements you earn and your progress toward them are sent to Apple and handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. The App has no leaderboards, and the Developer receives nothing from Game Center about you.

The game works the same if you are not signed in, and it keeps your progress on your device either way. You can sign out of Game Center, or change what it shares, in your device's settings.

Information collected automatically

The App does not collect usage statistics, device identifiers, IP addresses, or location, and it does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software. It has no account system of its own.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may provide the Developer with anonymous crash reports and performance data. You can turn this off at any time in the same settings screen.

Purchases

The App offers one optional in-app purchase, Unlock the Kingdom. It is processed by Apple through the App Store, and the Developer does not receive your payment information. The App asks Apple whether your Apple Account owns the purchase so it can unlock the kingdom again after a reinstall or on a new device.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your saved games stay on your device until you delete them. The App keeps up to three heroes in separate save slots, and you can delete one from the save slots screen; deleting the App removes them all. Because the Developer never receives your information, there is nothing held by the Developer to delete.
