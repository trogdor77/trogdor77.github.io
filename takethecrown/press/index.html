The tale

Why Take the Crown exists

Sir Leo is a real cat: the developer's own orange tabby. In the game, the King takes him, and the hero sets out to bring him home.

The meows and purrs in the game are recordings of the real Sir Leo, and the family's other pets appear in the game too. Everything else, from the heroes to the last torch in the castle, is drawn in code.

Alexander Rohr, founder and developer

The quest

How it plays

Pick a hero

Torvald or Brynja, with a name of the player's choosing and points to spend on four stats.

Fight

Swing, charge heavy attacks and block. A perfect block stuns the enemy for a double-damage counter.

Beat the boss

Every region ends in a boss fight, and every boss fights a second phase at half health.

Gear up

Back in the village: shops, jobs, mini games, and a home to rest in before heading out again.

The armory

What it does

Boss fights with a second phase

At half health each boss stops, roars, and comes back angrier with new moves.

Combat with timing

Light and charged swings, blocking, perfect blocks that stun, and arrows that can be deflected back.

Loot and gear

29 weapons with their own abilities, 15 shields and 13 suits of armor, with rare finds from bosses and chests.

A living village

Shops and shopkeepers, a daily job board and weekly challenge, an inn with stories to follow, and a home with a trophy wall.

Mini games

Jousting against five rival knights, fishing, the forge, the cauldron, and a yarn game with Sir Leo.

Enemy cards

A card for every enemy met, flipped to reveal strengths and weak spots.

Game Center

36 achievements, with progress shown on the counted ones.

No ads, no tracking

No account, no ads, no analytics, and it plays fully offline.

Descriptions

One line

Take the Crown is a side-scrolling action RPG for iPhone and iPad: the King took your cat, and you fight across six regions and their bosses to take him back, and the crown.

Short paragraph

Take the Crown is a side-scrolling action RPG for iPhone and iPad. When the King's guards carry off Sir Leo, the hero's cat, players pick up a sword and fight from the Rolling Hills to the King's own castle: 21 levels across six regions, each ending in a boss that fights a second phase at half health. Combat is about timing, with charged swings, blocking and perfect blocks that stun. Between battles, the village has shops, a job board, an inn with stories to follow, and mini games including jousting and fishing, and players collect 29 weapons and a card for every enemy. The first region is free, and a one-time purchase unlocks the rest of the kingdom. There are no ads, no accounts and no tracking. Take the Crown was built by independent developer Alexander Rohr, and Sir Leo is his family's real cat.
