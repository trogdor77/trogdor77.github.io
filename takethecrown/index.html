The story

A King who wants the finest of everything

It's a quiet morning in your cottage. You're rolling a ball of yarn for Sir Leo, your cat, when the door bursts open and the King's guards march in. The King wants the finest of everything, and he's decided that includes your cat.

So you pick up a sword. From the Rolling Hills to the King's own castle, you fight goblins, bandits and the King's knights, take on a boss at the end of every region, and come home stronger each time.

Sir Leo is our real cat. The meows and purrs you hear in the game are recordings of him, and the family's other pets make an appearance too.

Alexander Rohr, developer

How it plays

Fight, grow, go home

Pick your hero

Play as Torvald or Brynja, name them, and spend your first points on strength, endurance, stamina and dexterity.

Fight

Swing, charge heavy attacks and block. Perfect-block a blow to stun the enemy, then hit back twice as hard.

Beat the boss

Every region ends in a boss fight. At half health each boss gets angrier and fights a second phase.

Gear up in the village

Spend your gold on weapons, shields, armor and potions, take jobs from the board, rest at home, and head back out.

Features

A whole kingdom to fight through

Bosses with a second phase

The Goblin Chief, the Werewolf, the Frost Troll, the Warlord and the King's Champion each stop the fight at half health, roar, and come back angrier with new moves.

Blocking that matters

Block to soak up a hit, or time it perfectly to stun the enemy and turn the fight around. Arrows can be deflected right back.

34 weapons

Swords, axes, maces, greatswords, bows and crossbows, each with its own ability, plus 15 shields and 15 suits of armor. Carry a spare on your belt, and show off the rarest on the rack at home.

A village to call home

Visit the blacksmith and the armorer, brew potions, eat at the inn, take daily jobs from the board, and follow the innkeeper's stories for rare rewards.

Mini games

Joust against five rival knights, fish at the mill pond, work the forge, brew at the cauldron, and play with Sir Leo when you're home.

Collect every enemy card

Every enemy you meet adds a card to your collection. Flip it to learn its strengths and its weak spots.

Achievements

Earn 36 Game Center achievements, with progress shown as you go.

Made for both screens

Plays in landscape on iPhone and iPad, with settings for music, sound and vibration. The game pauses itself if a call comes in.

No ads, no tracking

No account, no ads and no analytics. The game works fully offline.
