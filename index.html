About

Why I build these apps

The apps I build come from moments where I, a friend, or a family member said, "I wish there was an app for that."

I got frustrated with how many apps are built around ads, so most of my apps favor a one-time purchase instead, with a free version so you can try before you buy. For Meduo, the core experience is free, with an optional subscription for advanced features. I'm also building casual games, with some fun influence from my daughters along the way.
