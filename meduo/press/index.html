Why Meduo exists

Since she was three, my daughter has had sudden episodes where she would vomit, then tell us the room was spinning.

We started keeping a journal of every episode: what happened, when, and how long it lasted. Sharing it with her pediatrician started a path through audiology and other specialists, all the way to a neurosurgeon. The final diagnosis was vestibular migraines.

That journal made the difference. It also showed me how often families are asked to remember things they can't: the medication list at a new doctor's office, the date of a past surgery, a child's vaccination history.

While I was building Meduo, I showed it to my in-laws, who care for my father-in-law's mother. Medication reminders and appointment tracking for her quickly became part of their routine. That's who Meduo is for: anyone keeping track of health for more than one person.

Alexander Rohr, founder and developer

Review of systems

What it does

Family profiles

A separate record for each person you care for, with one-tap switching.

Medication reminders

Reminders name who the dose is for, and doses due at the same time arrive as one notification. Scan a label's barcode to fill in details.

Health journal

Daily entries in categories you define: dizziness, sleep, mood, seizures, whatever your family needs to track.

Lab results and vital trends

Store results over time and chart blood pressure, weight, glucose, and more across 30, 90, and 365 days.

Appointments and procedures

Upcoming and past visits with calendar sync, plus surgeries with facility, surgeon, and outcome.

Vaccinations, conditions, allergies

Searchable catalogs with lot numbers, dates, and providers.

One-tap PDF summary

Export a complete health record to hand to a new doctor, a specialist, or the ER.

Private by design

No Meduo servers and no account. Data syncs through the user's own iCloud and is locked behind Face ID, Touch ID, or Optic ID.

Descriptions

One line

Meduo is a private health record app for iPhone and iPad that lets families track medications, appointments, and health history for everyone they care for.

Short paragraph

Meduo keeps health records for a whole family in one private place: children, yourself, and aging parents. It tracks medications with named reminders, appointments, lab results, vaccinations, surgeries, and a customizable symptom journal, and exports a complete PDF summary for any doctor. A Lock Screen widget works as an emergency card for first responders. There are no Meduo servers or accounts; data stays in the user's own iCloud. Meduo was built by independent developer Alexander Rohr after a symptom journal helped the developer's daughter get a diagnosis.
