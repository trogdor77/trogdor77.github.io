Private health records for your whole family: your kids, yourself, and the parents you care for.

Free to download · Premium $19.99/year · iPhone and iPad

Why Meduo

Built from a family's own journey

For years, my daughter had unexplained episodes of vomiting and vertigo. The journal we kept of every episode took her doctors through specialist after specialist, until she was finally diagnosed with vestibular migraines.

Meduo gives every family that kind of record, along with the medications, surgeries, and history a new doctor always asks about. My in-laws use it to care for an elderly parent. Whoever you look after, it keeps their health information in one place.

Alexander Rohr, developer

Features

Everything a doctor asks about

Family profiles

A separate record for each person you care for, with one-tap switching.

Medication reminders

Reminders say who the dose is for, and doses due at the same time arrive together. Scan a label's barcode to fill in details.

Health journal timeline

Entries have a time and are grouped by day. Add symptoms, the medications you gave, and a temperature, then see the days since each symptom and a fever chart. Import an existing diary from Apple Notes.

As-needed medications

Log a dose in a tap and see when it was last given, with an optional minimum time between doses.

Lab results and vital trends

Keep results over time and chart blood pressure, weight, glucose, and more.

Appointments and procedures

Upcoming and past visits with calendar sync, plus surgeries with facility, surgeon, and outcome.

Vaccinations, conditions, allergies

Searchable lists with dates, lot numbers, and providers.

One-tap PDF summary

Export a complete health record to share with a new doctor, a specialist, or the ER.

Doctor report

Turn a journal into a PDF for the dates you choose, with entry times, symptoms, temperatures, and medications given.

Search and attachments

Search your records for one person or the whole family, and attach photos and scanned documents to journal entries, medications, labs, and more.

Widgets

Today's medications and appointments on your Home Screen.

What's new

Latest update

Version 1.5

Your journal is now a timeline.

  • Journal timeline. Entries have a time and are grouped by day, so you can see what happened and when.
  • Medications given. Record the medication in a journal entry and it is added to that medication's dose history, so the Dashboard's "Last given" and your doctor report include it.
  • Temperature. Add a temperature in °F or °C. Fevers are tagged for you and shown on a fever chart with the medications given marked.
  • Symptom tags and patterns. Tags are suggested from what you write, with the days since each symptom and a symptom timeline chart.
  • As-needed medications. Log a dose from the Dashboard, see when it was last given, and set an optional minimum time between doses.
  • Doctor report. Create a PDF of a journal for the dates you choose, or email it.
  • Import from Apple Notes. Bring an existing diary in as a journal, one entry per dated line.
  • Search, photos, and scanned documents, plus quicker new entries.
  • Fixes: lists refresh right after you add a record, Meduo remembers which family member you were viewing, and a new journal entry no longer closes and reopens while you write it.

Privacy

Your records stay yours

Meduo has no account to create and no servers of its own. Your records are stored on your device and sync through your own iCloud, so they're available on your iPhone and iPad. Meduo never sees them.

Face ID, Touch ID, or Optic ID is required every time the app opens.
