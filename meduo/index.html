Why Meduo

Built from a family's own journey

For years, my daughter had unexplained episodes of vomiting and vertigo. The journal we kept of every episode took her doctors through specialist after specialist, until she was finally diagnosed with vestibular migraines.

Meduo gives every family that kind of record, along with the medications, surgeries, and history a new doctor always asks about. My in-laws use it to care for an elderly parent. Whoever you look after, it keeps their health information in one place.

Alexander Rohr, developer

Features

Everything a doctor asks about

Family profiles

A separate record for each person you care for, with one-tap switching.

Medication reminders

Reminders say who the dose is for, and doses due at the same time arrive together. Scan a label's barcode to fill in details.

Health journal

Daily entries in categories you choose: dizziness, sleep, mood, or anything else you need to track.

Lab results and vital trends

Keep results over time and chart blood pressure, weight, glucose, and more.

Appointments and procedures

Upcoming and past visits with calendar sync, plus surgeries with facility, surgeon, and outcome.

Vaccinations, conditions, allergies

Searchable lists with dates, lot numbers, and providers.

One-tap PDF summary

Export a complete health record to share with a new doctor, a specialist, or the ER.

Widgets

Today's medications and appointments on your Home Screen.

Lock Screen

An emergency card on your Lock Screen

Meduo's Lock Screen widget shows your name, blood type, allergies, and emergency contact. A first responder can read it without unlocking your phone.

Privacy

Your records stay yours

Meduo has no account to create and no servers of its own. Your records are stored on your device and sync through your own iCloud, so they're available on your iPhone and iPad. Meduo never sees them.

Face ID, Touch ID, or Optic ID is required every time the app opens.
