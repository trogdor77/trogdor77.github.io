Land the Plane

Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

In short

  • Your progress, purchases, and settings stay on your device.
  • The developer does not collect, receive, or have access to any of it.
  • Land the Plane has no account, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.
  • Achievements are handled by Apple Game Center, and signing in is optional.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to Land the Plane (the "App") for iPhone and iPad, created by Alexander Rohr (the "Developer"). The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Information stored on your device

Land the Plane stores your progress, such as the stars you have earned, your points and unlocked planes, smoke trails, and slingshots, your daily challenge progress, and your settings for music, sound, vibration, and pitch buttons, locally on your device using standard iOS storage. This information stays on the device it was created on; the App does not sync it to iCloud or any other device.

Land the Plane does not operate any servers, and the App does not send your progress or play activity to the Developer.

Game Center

Land the Plane can report your achievements to Apple Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, your earned achievements are sent to and shown by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The Developer does not receive them. The game works the same if you are not signed in, and it keeps your achievements on your device either way.

Information collected automatically

The App does not collect usage statistics, device identifiers, IP addresses, or location, and it does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software. It has no account system of its own.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may provide the Developer with anonymous crash reports and performance data. You can turn this off at any time in the same settings screen.

Purchases

The App offers one optional in-app purchase, Unlock All Worlds. It is processed by Apple through the App Store, and the Developer does not receive your payment information. The App asks Apple whether your Apple Account owns the purchase so it can unlock your worlds again after a reinstall or on a new device.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your progress stays on your device until you delete it. Deleting the App removes all locally stored progress. Because the Developer never receives your information, there is nothing held by the Developer to delete.

Links to other sites

The App's About screen includes a link to a web page about the project. Opening it takes you to that site in your browser, where that site's own privacy policy applies. This website may also link to other websites, which have their own policies.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about privacy in Land the Plane, email support@rohrsoftware.dev.
