Why Land the Plane

A phrase that stuck

I was sitting in the carpool line, waiting to pick up my daughter, on a business call that just kept going. Someone on the call would not wrap up. My wife, sitting next to me, finally said it out loud: “Land the plane.”

It was a comment that stuck with me, and it became the inspiration for the game. Every level is about the same thing: launch, fly, and finally bring it in for a gentle landing.

Alexander Rohr, developer

How it plays

One launch, one landing

Launch

Drag back anywhere on the screen and let go. The farther you pull, the harder the plane flies.

Fly

Hold the top of the screen to climb and the bottom to dive. Climbing costs speed, and diving gets it back.

Slow down

Tap flaps to bleed off speed for a short approach, then line up with the runway.

Land

Touch down gently on the runway to finish the level, and earn up to three stars for a clean landing.

Features

Easy to learn, hard to land

168 levels

Twelve levels in every world, from a gentle first flight to a night bonus, plus a legendary endurance flight.

Endurance flights

Three-star every level in a world to unlock its endurance flight: a long journey from day into night with the engine running. Hold the throttle, watch the fuel gauge, grab fuel cans, and don't climb too high or the engine stalls. A flag marks your best distance.

Hazards that fly back

Dodge lava bombs, ice chunks, ghosts, bats, asteroids, cannonballs, fighter jets, hawks, tornadoes, lightning, dragon fire, and a rock monster that throws boulders.

Runways that move

Land on a carrier at sea, a rolling train, a bobbing raft, or a floating ice floe, and catch the arrestor wire.

New planets, new gravity

In Space, each planet has its own gravity and air, so the same flying skills need a different touch.

A world of sound

Every world has its own soundscape, from birdsong and crashing waves to howling wind, city traffic, and bubbling lava. Hazards growl, cackle, and gloat when they get you.

Daily challenges

Three new challenges every day, easy, medium, and hard, pay out points. They're free for everyone.

Plane shop

Spend your points on nearly 90 plane skins, smoke trails, and slingshots, from common to legendary. Fly a dragon, a UFO, or a rainbow unicorn.

Achievements and leaderboards

Earn 46 Game Center achievements and compete on leaderboards for total stars, endurance distance, and daily challenges.

Made for both screens

Plays in landscape on iPhone and iPad, with settings for music, sound, vibration, and on-screen pitch buttons.

No ads, no tracking

No account, no ads, and no analytics. The game works fully offline.
