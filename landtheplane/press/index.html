Origin

Why Land the Plane exists

The idea came from a carpool line. Alexander was on a business call, waiting to pick up his daughter, and the call kept going. His wife, sitting next to him, said “Land the plane.”

The comment stuck with him, and it became the inspiration for a game built around bringing a plane in for a gentle landing.

Alexander Rohr, founder and developer

Briefing

How it plays

Launch

Drag back anywhere on the screen and let go. The farther the pull, the harder the plane flies.

Fly

Hold the top of the screen to climb and the bottom to dive. Climbing costs speed, and diving gets it back.

Slow down

Tap flaps to bleed off speed for a short approach.

Land

Touch down gently on the runway to finish the level, and earn up to three stars.

Systems

What it does

Slingshot launch

One drag sets the power. Then it is climb, dive, and flaps to line up the touchdown.

Hazards in every world

Lava bombs, ice chunks, ghosts, bats, asteroids, cannonballs, lightning, and tornadoes, depending on the world.

Runways that move

Carriers, trains, rafts, and floating ice floes, with an arrestor wire to catch.

New planets, new gravity

In Space, each planet has its own gravity and air.

Daily challenges

Three new challenges a day, easy, medium, and hard, that pay out points.

Plane shop

Spend earned points on more than 80 plane skins, smoke trails, and slingshots, from common to legendary.

Game Center achievements

47 achievements for landings, streaks, special runways, and night flights.

No ads, no tracking

No account, no ads, no analytics, and it plays fully offline.

Descriptions

One line

Land the Plane is an iPhone and iPad game where you launch a little plane with a slingshot and set it down gently on the runway, across 14 worlds.

Short paragraph

Land the Plane is a game for iPhone and iPad about one thing: landing. Players drag back to launch a little plane with a slingshot, then climb, dive, and use flaps to bring it down gently on the runway for up to three stars. 154 levels across 14 worlds mix in moving carriers, trains, and ice floes, lava bombs, ghosts, and asteroids, and even planets with their own gravity. The first three worlds are free, and a one-time purchase unlocks the rest along with daily challenges, a plane shop, and Game Center achievements. There are no ads, no accounts, and no tracking. Land the Plane was built by independent developer Alexander Rohr, and the name comes from a real moment: a business call that would not end, and his wife, sitting next to him in the carpool line, saying “land the plane.”
