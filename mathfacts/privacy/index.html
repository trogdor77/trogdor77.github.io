Math Facts: The Game

Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

In short

  • Player names, buddies, and progress stay on your devices and in your own iCloud account.
  • The developer does not collect, receive, or have access to any of it.
  • Math Facts has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and it works offline.
  • The Grown-ups area, purchases, and links out of the app are behind a parental gate.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to Math Facts: The Game (the "App") for iPhone and iPad, created by Alexander Rohr (the "Developer"). The App is made for children to use, with a grown-up area for parents and caregivers.

Information a grown-up enters

A parent or caregiver sets up one or more players in the App. For each player the App stores a name (any name the grown-up chooses), a buddy or a picture, a starting grade, settings such as which facts to practice and the practice timer, and the player's progress: answers, stars, coins, achievements, rounds played, and treasure map progress. A grown-up can also add reward ideas, such as "ice cream trip," with a star goal.

This information is stored on your device. If iCloud is turned on, it is also synced through your private iCloud database using Apple's CloudKit, so it is available on your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Data in your private iCloud database is managed by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The Developer cannot see or access it.

The App does not operate any servers, and it does not send any of this information to the Developer or to any third party.

Photos

A grown-up can choose a photo from the photo library as a player's picture or a background. The App uses the system photo picker, so it receives only the pictures you choose, and not the rest of your library. A chosen picture is stored with the player on your device and in your private iCloud, and it is not uploaded anywhere else.

Information collected automatically

The App does not collect usage statistics, device identifiers, IP addresses, or location, and it does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software. It does not ask for or use the advertising identifier.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may provide the Developer with anonymous crash reports and performance data. These reports do not include player names or progress. You can turn this off at any time in the same settings screen.

Notifications and other device features

The App does not show notifications. It uses Apple's background updates only so iCloud sync can keep your devices up to date. Sounds, music, and vibration can be turned off in the Grown-ups area. The App does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, or location.

Children's information

Math Facts is designed for children, so it is built to collect nothing from them. There are no accounts, no chat or social features, no ads, and no links that a child can follow out of the App. Children do not enter an email address, a phone number, or any other contact information. A player's name is typed by a grown-up and stays on the family's devices and in their iCloud account.

The Developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the Developer never receives player information, there is nothing held by the Developer to review or delete.

Parental gate

The Grown-ups area, the purchase screen, and the links to the Terms, this Privacy Policy, and Support are protected by a parental gate. To open them, you type a large number from its spelled-out words, which an adult can do and a young child cannot.

Purchases

The full version, as a monthly subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase, and the 14-day free trial are processed by Apple through the App Store. The Developer does not receive your payment information. The App learns only whether a purchase or trial is active, from Apple, so it can unlock the right features. With Family Sharing, Apple shares the purchase with your family group.

Keeping and deleting information

Information stays in the App until you delete it. A grown-up can delete a player in the Grown-ups area, which removes that player's progress. To remove everything, delete the App and remove Math Facts' data from iCloud in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Account Storage.

Security

Your data is protected by your device's security and by iCloud's encryption. No system is perfectly secure, so keep your device passcode and Apple Account protected.

Links to other sites

This website and the Grown-ups area of the App may link to other websites. Those sites are not operated by the Developer, and their own privacy policies apply.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
