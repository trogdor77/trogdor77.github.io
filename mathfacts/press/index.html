Teacher's note

Why Math Facts exists

Math Facts was built for a daughter who finds her math facts hard. Her dad, independent developer Alexander Rohr, wanted practice that would not make her feel bad about a wrong answer.

So a miss gets a silly sound and the right answer on screen, never a buzzer. Every fact lives on a map that fills in as it is mastered, and the rewards at the end are real ones that a grown-up sets, like an ice cream trip. The treasure map was her idea.

Alexander Rohr, founder and developer

How it plays

Pick a player

Each child has a buddy, a grade, a practice timer, and their own progress.

Answer

Rounds are 15 questions on a big number pad, with no timer unless one is turned on.

Learn from a miss

A gentle “Not quite!” shows the right answer, and Show me how adds a picture and steps.

Collect

Stars, coins, levels, achievements, and super buddies from the treasure map.

Subjects

What it does

Practice that adapts

Learns which facts each child knows and which need work, and spreads mastery over days so facts are remembered, not just answered.

Show me how

After a wrong answer, a step-by-step strategy with a picture: make a ten, near doubles, the nines trick, think times, and more, for every fact up to 12.

Wrong answers are no big deal

A missed fact gets a gentle “Not quite!” and the right answer on screen. Speed is a bonus, and there is no timer unless you turn one on.

Sign mix-up help

Notices when a child answers with the wrong sign, like 11 for 8 − 3, and points it out.

Big numbers in columns

Adding and taking away in columns like on paper, with carrying, borrowing, and the mistake shown in the right column.

Missing numbers

? + 5 = 12 and 3 × ? = 21: fact families the other way round.

Treasure map

Eight islands of stops to pass with 85% right, each ending in a treasure chest with a super buddy inside.

Achievements and levels

Nearly 100 achievements with progress to see, and level titles from Number Newbie to Grand Math Master.

Real rewards

Grown-ups set rewards like an ice cream trip. Children earn stars and choose where to put them.

Progress report

Practice time, accuracy, facts known, and the facts that need practice, for each child.

A profile for each child

Their own buddy, background, grade, practice timer, and progress.

Made for iPhone and iPad

Portrait on iPhone, and portrait or landscape on iPad, with the screens scaled up to fill the bigger display.

iCloud sync

Progress syncs through your family's own private iCloud. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Descriptions

One line

Math Facts: The Game is an iPhone and iPad app that makes math fact practice feel like a game for kids in grades 1 to 4 and beyond, with a treasure map of eight islands, super buddies to win, and real rewards that a grown-up sets.

Short paragraph

Math Facts: The Game helps kids practice adding, taking away, times tables, and dividing on a big number pad, in short rounds with no timer unless a grown-up turns one on. A wrong answer gets a gentle “Not quite!” and a Show me how card with a picture, never a buzzer. Kids pass stops on a treasure map to win super buddies, earn stars, coins, levels, and nearly 100 achievements, and put their stars toward rewards a grown-up chooses, like an ice cream trip. Grown-ups pick the grade and which facts to focus on and see a progress report for each child. There is no account, no ads, and no analytics, and progress syncs only through the family's own iCloud. Math Facts was built by independent developer Alexander Rohr for his daughter, who finds math facts hard.
