Why BudgetXP+

Twenty years in the making

Twenty years ago, I wrote a Windows desktop app so my wife and I could track our spending and set money aside for a rainy day. It worked, but only at the computer. A purchase made at the store had to be remembered and entered later.

BudgetXP+ is the fix for that: a budget that lives in your pocket, so a transaction gets logged the moment it happens. BudgetXP+ Desktop came next, syncing over iCloud so our accounts, budgets, and transactions stay in step everywhere we go: the best of both worlds.

Alexander Rohr, developer

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Account management

Track checking, savings, money market, CDs, credit cards, and loans. Hide closed accounts and view totals at a glance.

Transaction tracking

Log expenses and income in seconds with categories, autocomplete descriptions, and a smart duplicate detector.

Receipt scanning

Photograph a receipt and let the app read the total, merchant, and card number automatically.

Budget goals

Set monthly spending limits per category and track progress with live, color-coded cards on the dashboard.

Spending reports

Visualize spending with donut charts, monthly trends, and a running net worth view.

Recurring transactions

Schedule bills and income to repeat weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or annually, and post them with a single tap.

Account transfers

Record transfers between accounts and keep every balance accurate automatically.

CSV export

Export your full transaction history and share it with any app or spreadsheet.

Bill reminders

Get notified on each recurring bill's due date, at a time you choose.

Built for iPhone & iPad

Full iPad Split View support, automatic light and dark mode, and customizable categories with an icon picker.

Biometric lock

Protect your financial data with Face ID or Touch ID. The app locks automatically when sent to the background.

Also on Mac

BudgetXP+ Desktop is available separately on the Mac App Store, syncing automatically over iCloud.

Privacy

Your money stays yours

BudgetXP+ has no account to create and no servers of its own. Your accounts and transactions are stored on your device and sync through your own iCloud, so they're available on your iPhone, iPad, and Mac. BudgetXP+ never sees them.

Face ID or Touch ID can lock the app every time it opens.
