Why BudgetXP+ exists

Twenty years ago, I wrote a Windows desktop app so my wife and I could track our spending and set money aside for a rainy day.

It worked, but only at the computer. A purchase made at the store had to be remembered and entered later, if it got entered at all.

BudgetXP+ is the fix for that: a budget that lives in your pocket, so a transaction gets logged the moment it happens. BudgetXP+ Desktop came next, syncing over iCloud so our accounts, budgets, and transactions stay in step everywhere we go — the best of both worlds.

Alexander Rohr, founder and developer

Line items

What it does

Account management

Track checking, savings, money market, CDs, credit cards, and loans. Hide closed accounts and view totals at a glance.

Transaction tracking

Log expenses and income in seconds with categories, autocomplete descriptions, and a smart duplicate detector.

Receipt scanning

Photograph a receipt and let the app read the total, merchant, and card number automatically.

Budget goals

Set monthly spending limits per category and track progress with live, color-coded cards on the dashboard.

Spending reports

Visualize spending with donut charts, monthly trends, and a running net worth view.

Recurring transactions

Schedule bills and income to repeat weekly, bi-weekly, monthly, or annually, and post them with a single tap.

Account transfers

Record transfers between accounts and keep every balance accurate automatically.

CSV export

Export your full transaction history and share it with any app or spreadsheet.

Private by design

No BudgetXP+ servers and no account. Data syncs through the user's own iCloud and can be locked behind Face ID or Touch ID.

Descriptions

One line

BudgetXP+ is a personal finance app for iPhone and iPad that lets you track accounts, set budgets, and understand your spending.

Short paragraph

BudgetXP+ brings everyday budgeting into your pocket. It tracks checking, savings, credit card, and loan accounts, logs transactions in seconds with receipt scanning and duplicate detection, and sets monthly budget goals with live progress tracking. Recurring bills and income post with a single tap, and spending reports chart exactly where the money goes. There are no BudgetXP+ servers or accounts; data stays in the user's own iCloud and syncs automatically with the companion BudgetXP+ Desktop app for Mac. BudgetXP+ was built by independent developer Alexander Rohr, who wrote an earlier version of the same idea as a Windows desktop app twenty years ago.
