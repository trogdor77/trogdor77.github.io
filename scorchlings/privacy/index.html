Scorchlings

Privacy Policy

Effective November 25, 2026

In short

  • Your progress, embers, power-ups, purchases and settings stay on your device.
  • The developer does not collect, receive, or have access to any of it.
  • Scorchlings has no account, no ads, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.
  • Leaderboards and achievements are handled by Apple Game Center, and signing in is optional.

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to Scorchlings (the "App"), a puzzle game for iPhone and iPad created by Alexander Rohr (the "Developer"). The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Information stored on your device

Scorchlings stores your game locally on your device using standard iOS storage. This includes the levels you have cleared, your lives, embers and power-ups, your Daily Treasure calendar and any Hearth Fire time, the achievements you have earned and the counts behind them (such as tiles burned and your win streak), whether you own the full game, the "New!" tips you have seen, and your music and sound settings. This information stays on the device it was created on; the App does not sync it to iCloud or any other device.

Scorchlings does not operate any servers, and the App does not send your progress or play activity to the Developer.

Game Center

Scorchlings can report scores and achievements to Apple Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores on three leaderboards (levels cleared, embers earned by playing, and best win streak), the achievements you earn and your progress toward them are sent to Apple and handled under Apple's Privacy Policy. Other players may see your Game Center nickname and scores, depending on your Game Center settings. The Developer receives nothing from Game Center about you beyond what Apple shows on its public leaderboards.

The game works the same if you are not signed in, and it keeps your progress on your device either way. You can sign out of Game Center, or change what it shares, in your device's settings.

Information collected automatically

The App does not collect usage statistics, device identifiers, IP addresses, or location, and it does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking software. It has no account system of its own, and it does not ask for access to your photos, camera, microphone, contacts, location or notifications.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device's settings (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may provide the Developer with anonymous crash reports and performance data. You can turn this off at any time in the same settings screen.

Purchases

The App offers optional in-app purchases: the full game, and ember packs. They are processed by Apple through the App Store, and the Developer does not receive your payment information. The App asks Apple whether your Apple Account owns the full game so it can open levels 101 to 1,000 again after a reinstall or on a new device.

Ratings

Now and then, after a win, the App may ask Apple to show its standard rating prompt. Any rating or review you give goes to Apple and the App Store, not through the App.

Keeping and deleting your information

Your progress stays on your device until you delete the App, which removes it. Because the Developer never receives your information, there is nothing held by the Developer to delete. Scores and achievements already sent to Game Center are kept by Apple; you can manage them in Game Center.

Links to other sites

The App does not link to other websites. This website may link to other websites, which have their own policies.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about privacy in Scorchlings, email support@rohrsoftware.dev.
