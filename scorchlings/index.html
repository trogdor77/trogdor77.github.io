Why I made it

A color puzzle without the ads

Color shooter puzzles are some of the most relaxing games on a phone, and most of them stop you every few levels for an ad. I wanted one that didn't, so I made my own, with dragons, since a dragon's eye is the Rohr Software logo.

Scorchlings has 1,000 levels, each one checked by a solver so it can always be won without power-ups. The first 100 are free, and one purchase opens the rest. There are no ads and there never will be.

Alexander Rohr, developer

How it plays

Pick a dragon, watch it burn

Send a dragon

Tap the dragon at the front of a column. It flies around the picture on the belt.

Burn its color

At each spot it breathes at the first tile it can see. If the tile is its color, it burns, and the dragon uses one puff.

Roost or fly home

A dragon out of puffs flies home. One with fire left lands in a roost, and you can send it out again later.

Clear the picture

Burn every tile to win. If a dragon comes back and every roost is full, the level is lost.

Features

More than matching colors

Chained dragons

Two or three dragons linked by a gold chain fly together, but only once they're all at the front of their columns.

Mystery and frozen dragons

Mystery dragons hide their color until they reach the front. Frozen dragons thaw a little with every dragon you send.

Stone walls

Fire stops at stone, so walls shield the tiles behind them. Only a Fireball smashes through.

Shades that fool the eye

Later pictures fill the whole board and use light and dark shades of the same color, so read carefully before you send a dragon.

Five power-ups

Undo (one free every level), Pluck any dragon from the queue, drop a Fireball anywhere, Unchain a group, or add an Extra Roost. Every level can be won without them.

Daily Treasure

A gift every day you play, and a hoard chest on Day 7. Miss a day and your place is kept. Your first win each day earns double embers.

Hearth Fire

You have five lives, and one comes back every 30 minutes. Hearth Fire from the Daily Treasure gives unlimited lives for a while.

Game Center

Three leaderboards (levels cleared, embers earned and best win streak) and 28 achievements. Bought embers never count.

No ads, no tracking

No ads, no account and no analytics. Your progress stays on your device, and the game plays fully offline.
