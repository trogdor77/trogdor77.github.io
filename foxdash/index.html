Why Fox Dash

A father-daughter project

My daughter has a love for foxes, so we decided to make a game together. What started as one fox grew into more than 30 characters, from other fox species all the way to dragons.

It was a fun project to build with her, and we wanted to share it with the world.

Alexander Rohr, developer

Features

Simple to play, hard to master

One-tap controls

Tap to jump, double tap to double jump. Timing and knowing each world's obstacles separate a good run from a great one.

30+ characters

Play as a fox, raccoon, bear, penguin, husky, T-Rex, and many more. Reach score milestones to unlock new characters, free, just by playing.

Legendary dragons

The rarest characters in the game. Earn enough points to unlock the Royal Dragon, Ice Dragon, Storm Dragon, Lava Dragon, Shadow Dragon, and more.

Daily challenges

New missions every day. Complete them to earn star points and unlock even more characters without spending a dime.

Game Center

Leaderboards for every world, plus achievements to hunt down.

Privacy

No ads, no tracking

Fox Dash has no account, no ads, and no third-party analytics. Your progress stays on your device. Leaderboards and achievements are handled entirely by Apple Game Center, and signing in is optional.
