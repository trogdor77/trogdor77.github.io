Features

Simple to play, hard to master

One-tap controls

Tap to jump, and learn each world's twist: dive in the ocean, duck on the ice, fly with a fire button in Fantasy, or double jump in the park. Timing separates a good run from a great one.

37 characters

Play as a fox, raccoon, bear, penguin, husky, T-Rex, and many more. Reach score milestones to unlock new characters, free, just by playing.

Legendary dragons

Eight dragons that glide and roar. Earn points to unlock the Royal, Rose, Ice, Storm, Lava, and Shadow dragons, or pick up the Crystal Dragon in the Store.

Daily challenges

Four new missions every day, from easy to bonus. Complete them to earn star points and unlock more characters without spending a dime.

Game Center

Leaderboards for every world, plus 14 achievements to hunt down.

Privacy

No ads, no tracking

Fox Dash has no account, no ads, and no third-party analytics. Your progress stays on your device. Leaderboards and achievements are handled entirely by Apple Game Center, and signing in is optional.
