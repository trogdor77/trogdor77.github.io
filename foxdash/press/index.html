Why Fox Dash exists

My daughter has a love for foxes, so we decided to make a game together.

What started as one fox grew into more than 30 characters, from other fox species all the way to dragons, spread across 10 worlds she helped shape.

It was a fun project to build with her, and we wanted to share it with the world.

Alexander Rohr, founder and developer

What it does

One-tap controls

Tap to jump, double tap to double jump. Timing and knowing each world's obstacles separate a good run from a great one.

30+ characters

Play as a fox, raccoon, bear, penguin, husky, T-Rex, and many more, each unlocked by reaching score milestones.

Legendary dragons

The rarest characters in the game. Earn enough points to unlock the Royal Dragon, Ice Dragon, Storm Dragon, Lava Dragon, Shadow Dragon, and more.

Daily challenges

New missions every day, earning star points toward characters without spending a dime.

Game Center

Leaderboards for every world, plus achievements to hunt down.

No ads, no tracking

A one-time purchase, no account, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

Descriptions

One line

Fox Dash is a fast-paced endless runner for iPhone and iPad across 10 beautifully crafted worlds, with 30+ characters to unlock.

Short paragraph

Fox Dash is an endless runner across 10 worlds, from forests and deserts to outer space and a fantasy realm, each with its own obstacles. Players unlock 30+ characters, from foxes and raccoons to legendary dragons, by reaching score milestones or completing daily challenges. Game Center leaderboards and achievements add friendly competition. There are no ads, no accounts, and no tracking; it's a one-time purchase. Fox Dash was built by independent developer Alexander Rohr and his daughter, a lifelong fox fan, as a project the two of them made together.
