What it is

An editor for the everyday jobs

Scumble is for the quick work that doesn't need a heavyweight editor: cropping a screenshot, touching up a photo, adding a caption or a speech bubble, cutting something out, or making a small piece of artwork.

It keeps your picture in layers, so you can change your mind, and everything you do can be undone. It works on your Mac alone, with no account to create and no internet connection needed.

Features

Built natively for the Mac

Layers

Add, duplicate, merge, reorder by dragging, hide, and set each layer's opacity and blend mode.

Selections

Rectangle and ellipse selections. Cut, copy, paste, fill, erase, or crop to the selection, and painting stays inside it.

Paint and fill

A paintbrush and eraser with adjustable width, and a paint bucket with a tolerance setting.

Text, bubbles, and pills

Add plain text, a speech bubble, or a pill. Choose the size, bold, text color, and background color, and drag it into place.

Twelve adjustments

Curves, Levels, Hue / Saturation, Exposure, Brightness / Contrast, and more, each with a live preview on your picture.

Forty effects

Blurs, distortions, noise, glow, sketches, fractals, and more in nine categories, with a live preview and Repeat Last Effect.

A palette in every window

Each window has its own color, a ready palette, and a place for your custom colors, with a hue, opacity, and hex editor.

Size and canvas

Resize the image, change the canvas size from any anchor, flip, rotate, and flatten.

PNG, JPEG, and layers

Open and save PNG and JPEG, or save a Scumble file to keep every layer for later.

Import from iPhone or iPad

Bring in a photo or a scan from your nearby iPhone or iPad with Continuity Camera.

Privacy

Your images stay yours

Scumble has no account, no ads, no analytics, and no network connection. It opens and saves only the files you choose, and the developer never sees your images.
